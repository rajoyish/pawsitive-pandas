# Character Consistency Rule

When writing or generating prompts for image or video generation in this project, you MUST use the exact character descriptors and style locks provided below. Do not paraphrase or alter these descriptions, as exact wording is required to maintain character consistency across different scenes.

## 1. Style Lock (Include in EVERY prompt)
Always append or integrate this exact visual style description:
"3D animated premium studio shot, high-quality 3D CGI rendering, ultra-realistic soft fur texture, cinematic lighting, soft warm studio illumination highlighting individual strands of fur."

## 2. Papa panda (Father / Blue Male panda)
When Papa panda is in the scene, include this EXACT text:
"a male panda entirely covered in thick, fluffy, black and white fur with a smooth white face and black eye patches, round plump body, large expressive cartoon eyes, dark arched eyebrows, small round nose, wide cheerful toothy smile with square white teeth, and round black ears on top of his head."

## 3. Mama panda (Mother / Mama panda)
When Mama panda is in the scene, include this EXACT text:
"a Mama panda covered in thick, fluffy, black and white fur with a smooth white face and black eye patches, round plump body, large expressive cartoon eyes, dark arched eyebrows, small round nose, wide cheerful toothy smile with square white teeth, and round black ears on top of her head."

## 4. Babu panda (Son / Mint Baby panda)
When Babu panda is in the scene, include this EXACT text:
"a tiny baby panda covered in thick, fluffy, black and white fur with a smooth white face and tiny black eye patches, extremely round chubby body, oversized expressive cartoon eyes, small button nose, cute playful smile, and round black ears on top of its head."

## Rules for Prompt Construction:
1. Never change the fur colors, facial features, or body shapes described above.
2. When multiple characters are in a scene, separate their descriptions clearly. (e.g., "On the left, [Papa panda's exact text]. On the right, [Mama panda's exact text].").
3. Only change their actions, emotions, props, and environments in the prompt.
4. For video prompts, strictly exclude dialogue. Rely on background music and sound effects to communicate universally to a global audience.
